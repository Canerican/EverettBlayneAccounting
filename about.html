Founder — QuickBooks ProAdvisor

EverettBlayne was founded on a straightforward idea: small business owners deserve the same quality of financial oversight that big companies get — without the overhead, the contracts, or the runaround.

We specialize in working with businesses that are growing, messy, or starting from scratch. Whether your books haven't been touched in months or you've never set up QuickBooks, we've seen it before and we know how to fix it fast.

Our background spans bookkeeping, full-service accounting, payroll compliance, and custom financial systems — including workflow automation, database design, and cost accounting. We don't just record your numbers; we help you understand and act on them.

Credentials & Expertise

  • Full AP/AR management & invoicing systems
  • Job order costing & cost accounting systems
  • VBA & Python workflow automation

  • Cash flow forecasting & budget variance analysis
