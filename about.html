About EverettBlayne

Outsourced accounting, held to a financial institution standard.

EverettBlayne is an outsourced bookkeeping and accounting practice for businesses that want their books handled correctly without hiring in-house staff. I take care of cleanup, setup, monthly bookkeeping, and payroll, and deliver statements your CPA, lender, and leadership can rely on.

My background is in financial institution accounting: general ledgers, account reconciliations, regulatory reporting, and audit prep, where the numbers have to be right every time. Every client's books get that same level of rigor.

EverettBlayne is based in Norman, Oklahoma, and works with businesses in person around the OKC metro and remotely. You work directly with me, the person doing your books, with no rotating team and no handoffs.
