Questions about cleanup

How far back can you go?

As far as you need. Most cleanups cover the current year plus any open prior year your CPA needs. If you're several years behind, we'll prioritize what's needed for taxes first.

Do I need to be using QuickBooks?

No. If you're on another system or nothing at all, I can set up QuickBooks Online and build your books from your bank and credit card statements.

What do you need from me?

Usually just bank and credit card statements (or read-only access), plus answers to a short list of questions about transactions I can't identify. I'll keep requests to a minimum.

How long does a cleanup take?

It depends on how far behind you are and how many transactions you have. Your quote includes a realistic timeline, and you'll get updates as I work.

Will you file my back taxes?

No. I don't prepare tax returns. I get your books accurate and complete so your CPA or tax preparer can file quickly, and I'm happy to work with them directly.

Is my financial information secure?

Yes. I work through QuickBooks' own user access and secure document sharing, and I never ask you to email passwords. You own your QuickBooks file and data at all times.
