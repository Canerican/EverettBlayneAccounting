Common questions

Straight answers to what most people ask before getting started.

Are you a CPA?

No. I have a bachelor's degree in accounting, I'm a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor, and I work full-time as an accountant at a credit union. I handle bookkeeping, cleanup, payroll, and QuickBooks work, and I keep your books ready for your CPA or tax preparer. I don't prepare tax returns or perform audits.

My books are a mess. Can you still help?

Yes, and it's one of the most common situations I see. I'll clean up and reconcile your records first, then set up a simple system so you stay current. It's never too late to get organized.

How much does a cleanup cost?

It depends on how far behind you are and how much needs fixing. After a free review, you'll get one flat price and a timeline before any work starts.

Do I need to switch accounting software?

No. I work with what you already use. If you're starting from scratch, I'll recommend and set up QuickBooks Online as part of onboarding.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Most work happens remotely through secure QuickBooks access and shared documents. If you're in or around Norman and would rather meet in person, that works too.

What if I only need help for a month or two?

That's fine. Nothing is under contract, so you can stop, pause, or change what you need anytime.

Do you charge extra for payroll or invoicing?

No surprise add-ons. Your monthly price covers what we agree on up front, including payroll or invoicing if you need them. If your needs change, we'll talk before anything on your bill changes.

Can I get help with just one QuickBooks problem?

Yes. Book a free call and tell me what's going on. You'll get a price before I start.

What industries do you work with?

Most kinds of small businesses: contractors and trades, e-commerce, professional services, retail, and more. If you're not sure we're a fit, book a free call and I'll tell you honestly.
