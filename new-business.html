Avoid the mistakes that lead to cleanups

Each of these is common, and each one is avoidable.

  • Personal and business spending mixed together.
  • Startup costs that never got recorded.
  • Categories that don't make sense to your CPA.
  • Receipts you can't find at tax time.
  • No clear picture of whether you're making money.
  • A scramble when a lender asks for financials.

Every one of these is easier to prevent than to fix. That's what a good setup is for.

Questions about setup

Should I open a separate business bank account?

Yes. Keeping business and personal money separate is the single biggest thing you can do for clean books. If you haven't yet, do that first and I'll set up from there.

Which QuickBooks plan do I need?

It depends on things like whether you track inventory, run payroll, or need more than one user. I'll recommend the simplest plan that fits, so you're not paying for features you won't use.

Do I have to keep paying you every month after setup?

No. Plenty of owners keep their own books after a good setup. If you'd rather hand it off, monthly bookkeeping is there whenever you want it.

Can you register my business or file my taxes?

No. I don't form businesses, give legal advice, or prepare tax returns. I set up your books so your attorney, CPA, or tax preparer has what they need.
