Precision Payroll Processing

Your team is your most valuable asset. We ensure they are paid accurately and on time, while keeping your business perfectly compliant with all local, state, and federal tax regulations.

System Setup & Integration

We handle the heavy lifting of getting your payroll system online, ensuring it communicates flawlessly with your accounting software.

  • Advanced QuickBooks Online Payroll setup.
  • Secure integration with your business banking modules.
  • Employee onboarding, W-4 collection, and direct deposit configuration.

Full-Service Processing

Never miss a pay period. We run your payroll on your schedule, whether that is weekly, bi-weekly, or semi-monthly.

  • Accurate calculation of hourly wages, overtime, and salaries.
  • Management of PTO, sick leave, and custom deductions (benefits, garnishments).
  • Seamless direct deposit execution and paystub delivery.

Tax Compliance & Filings

Payroll tax penalties are steep. We take over the liability of calculating and submitting your tax payments so you never have to worry about a missed deadline.

  • Automated federal, state, and local tax withholdings.
  • Filing of quarterly payroll tax forms (Form 941, state unemployment).
  • Year-end W-2 generation and distribution.

Contractor Management

If you use freelancers or independent contractors, we ensure they are paid reliably alongside your standard W-2 employees.

  • W-9 collection and verified onboarding workflows.
  • Direct deposit payments for external vendors.
  • Year-end 1099-NEC generation and IRS reporting.
